## Spacing & Information Density Rules

- **Information Density:** Use medium-to-compact density. Avoid oversized hero elements, huge whitespace gaps, or tall cards.
- **Card Padding:** Set card containers to `p-4` or `p-5` instead of `p-6` or `p-8`.
- **Typography Sizes:**
  - Page Title ("Dashboard"): `text-xl` or `text-2xl` font-bold (currently too large).
  - Primary Metric/Values: `text-xl` or `text-2xl` font-bold.
  - Labels & Subtitles: `text-xs` font-medium text-muted.
- **Form Controls & Action Buttons:** Use standard `h-9` or `h-10` height with `text-sm` typography and `px-3` or `px-4` padding.
- **Layout Constraints:** Maximum page content container width should be `max-w-6xl` with `gap-4` grid spacing.
